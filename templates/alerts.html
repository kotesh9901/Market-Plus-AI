How price alerts work

Choose an asset and set your target price. Market Plus AI monitors the configured asset and triggers the alert when its price reaches or crosses your target.

♧

Automatic notifications

Every new alert is configured for in-app, email, and SMS notifications automatically. In-app and email delivery will be available first. SMS delivery will be activated after the SMS service integration is completed.

ⓘ

Important: Price alerts are informational tools, not guarantees of execution or profit. Market prices can change rapidly. Data delays, service interruptions, and market closures may affect when an alert is detected. Always verify prices before making a financial decision.
